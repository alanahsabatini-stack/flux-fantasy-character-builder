Karma Specialty

Your Karma Specialty is the backbone of all your decisions in the game and shapes how your character will behave. It determines their skills, influences their personality, and affects their stats, such as Med Kit effectiveness and their Karma Pool.

Karma Study

The Study is the heart of character creation in Flux Fantasy. After you pick a Specialty, you dive into a specific Study that defines your Powers and abilities. Each Study has unique advantages and helps shape your character's journey.
